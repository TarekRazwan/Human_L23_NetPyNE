=== Seed Propagation Verification ===
Date: 2026-06-05 19:08

This file proves that sweeping GLOBALSEED in batch.py correctly
propagates to all four NetPyNE seed channels (conn, stim, loc, cell)
and the OU noise path (SEED = GLOBALSEED * 10000).

The dual-derivation mechanism:
  1. cfg.py: seeds derived from GLOBALSEED (handles standalone runs)
  2. init.py: re-derives seeds from cfg.GLOBALSEED after loading cfg
     (catches batch runs where JSON snapshot has stale seeds)

Verification simulates the full batch->JSON->init pipeline:
  - batch loads cfg.py, overrides GLOBALSEED, saves to JSON
  - init.py loads JSON, re-derives seeds from GLOBALSEED

--- GLOBALSEED = 1234 ---
  After cfg.py load:
    cfg.GLOBALSEED = 1234
    cfg.seeds      = {'conn': 1234, 'stim': 1234, 'loc': 1234, 'cell': 1234}
  After batch override (GLOBALSEED = 1234):
    cfg.GLOBALSEED = 1234
    cfg.seeds      = {'conn': 1234, 'stim': 1234, 'loc': 1234, 'cell': 1234}
  After JSON round-trip (batch saves, init.py loads):
    cfg.GLOBALSEED = 1234
    cfg.seeds      = {'cell': 1234, 'conn': 1234, 'loc': 1234, 'stim': 1234}
  After init.py re-derivation (safety net):
    cfg.seeds      = {'conn': 1234, 'stim': 1234, 'loc': 1234, 'cell': 1234}
    OU SEED        = 12340000  (GLOBALSEED * 10000)

  RESULT: IDENTICAL to original baseline.
    conn=1234, stim=1234, loc=1234, cell=1234, OU=12340000
